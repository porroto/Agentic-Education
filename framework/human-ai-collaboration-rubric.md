# Human-AI Collaboration Rubric

| Dimension | 1 - Emerging | 2 - Developing | 3 - Proficient | 4 - Advanced |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Uses AI without clear goal | Has a general goal | Uses AI toward a clear learning goal | Defines a meaningful, ethical, real-world goal |
| Transparency | Does not disclose AI use | Mentions AI generally | Explains how AI was used | Explains AI use, limits, and human decisions |
| Judgment | Accepts AI output without review | Makes minor edits | Evaluates and revises AI output | Critiques, rejects, improves, and justifies choices |
| Evidence | Provides final product only | Provides limited process evidence | Provides drafts, notes, and revisions | Provides rich evidence showing growth over time |
| Reflection | Minimal explanation | Describes what happened | Explains learning and changes | Analyzes decisions, mistakes, and next steps |
