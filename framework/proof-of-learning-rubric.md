# Proof-of-Learning Rubric

| Dimension | 1 - Beginning | 2 - Approaching | 3 - Meeting | 4 - Exceeding |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Claim | Learning claim is unclear | Claim is present but broad | Claim is clear and connected to mission | Claim is precise, meaningful, and transferable |
| Evidence | Little evidence | Some evidence but incomplete | Evidence supports learning claim | Multiple evidence types show growth and revision |
| Reasoning | Minimal explanation | Basic explanation | Explains how evidence supports learning | Connects evidence to concepts, decisions, and impact |
| Revision | No revision shown | Revision mentioned | Revision is documented | Revision shows learning from feedback and failure |
| Validation | No human validation | Informal validation | Teacher or peer validation included | Multiple validators or community connection included |
