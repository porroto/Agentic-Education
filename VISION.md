# Vision

OpenLab exists because the future of education is not about proving humans did not use AI. It is about proving humans grew while using intelligence responsibly.

## Mission

Build a human-centered proof-of-learning ecosystem where students complete real-world missions, collect evidence, reflect on their process, receive human validation, and build portfolios that communicate growth over time.

## Core principle

> AI assists. Humans govern. Evidence speaks. Communities validate.

## North Star

When intelligence becomes abundant, human agency becomes the curriculum.

## What OpenLab protects

- Student dignity
- Teacher judgment
- Human authorship
- Learning evidence
- Privacy and consent
- Reflection and revision
- Community validation
- Non-financial recognition of learning

## What OpenLab rejects

- Automated high-stakes grading
- Student surveillance
- Unverified AI authority
- Financialized K-12 learning tokens
- Hidden AI decision-making
- Treating final products as the only evidence of learning
