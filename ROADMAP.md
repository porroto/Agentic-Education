## Phase 1.5: Governance Gate

Before substantial student-facing automation, explicitly answer:

- [ ] Who owns and controls each Evidence Set?
- [ ] Who may see it and for what purpose?
- [ ] What may AI infer from it?
- [ ] What must AI never infer?
- [ ] How can a learner challenge or correct an AI suggestion?
- [ ] When does evidence expire, archive, or become withdrawable?
- [ ] Who is authorized to perform Human Validation?
- [ ] What is the minimum evidence necessary for the Learning Claim?

**Gate invariant:**

> **AI REVIEW ≠ HUMAN VALIDATION**
