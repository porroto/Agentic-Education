# Lambda Research Grant Proposal

## OpenLab Proof-of-Learning: Human-Centered AI Evaluation of K-12 STEM Evidence

**Applicant:** Roger Vargas  
**Role:** Elementary STEM Educator; Founder, S.A.T. Labs / OpenLab  
**Requested Support:** $5,000 in Lambda Cloud credits  
**Project Duration:** 12-16 weeks

## Project Summary

OpenLab is a K-12 proof-of-learning research pilot designed to explore how artificial intelligence can support teachers in reviewing real-world STEM learning evidence without replacing teacher judgment.

The central research question is:

> Can AI assist teachers in organizing, reviewing, and giving feedback on K-12 project-based learning evidence while preserving human judgment, student agency, and transparent rubric-based assessment?

Lambda Cloud credits would support the development and evaluation of an AI-assisted evidence review prototype using de-identified or synthetic student learning artifacts.

## Expected Deliverables

- OpenLab Evidence Review Agent prototype
- Rubric-aligned proof-of-learning framework
- Responsible AI and privacy workflow
- Metadata schema for non-financial learning badges
- Technical report
- GitHub repository with privacy-safe examples
- Teacher-facing implementation templates

## Responsible AI Statement

OpenLab is designed as a teacher-in-the-loop system. AI outputs are suggestions only, not final grades or decisions. The project will use synthetic or de-identified artifacts and will not store personally identifiable student information.
