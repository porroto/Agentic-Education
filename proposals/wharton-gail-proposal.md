# Proposal for Wharton Generative AI Labs (GAIL)

## OpenLab: A Proof-of-Learning Framework for Human Agency in the Age of Generative AI

**Submitted by:** Roger Vargas  
**Founder:** S.A.T. Labs / OpenLab  
**Role:** K-12 STEM Educator  
**Research Areas:** Generative AI in Education, Human-AI Collaboration, Learning Evidence, Agentic Education

## Executive Summary

Generative AI is rapidly changing how students create, research, write, design, and solve problems. As AI capabilities increase, education faces a new challenge: If artificial intelligence can generate almost any artifact, how do we recognize authentic human learning?

OpenLab proposes a human-centered proof-of-learning framework that studies how students, educators, and AI systems can collaborate while preserving human agency, creativity, judgment, and growth.

Rather than asking whether students used AI, OpenLab asks a deeper question: How can we verify the learning journey that happened with AI?

## Alignment With Wharton GAIL

OpenLab aligns with Wharton GAIL's focus on research, prototyping, learning, AI applications, simulations, and the creative use of generative AI.

OpenLab adds a complementary trust layer:

> After AI creates powerful learning experiences, how do we verify human growth?

## Prototype: OpenLab Learning Evidence Agent

The prototype is a teacher-in-the-loop AI system that helps organize evidence from student projects.

Inputs:

- project artifacts
- reflections
- design iterations
- research notes
- prototypes
- feedback cycles

Outputs:

- learning timeline
- evidence map
- reflection prompts
- portfolio summaries
- skill connections
- teacher review packets

The system does not grade students. It helps humans see learning.

## Closing Statement

The future of AI in education should not only ask: What can AI create?

It should also ask: What kind of humans are we helping learners become?
