# OpenLab Manifesto

OpenLab exists because the future of education is not about proving humans did not use AI.

It is about proving humans grew while using intelligence responsibly.

When answers become abundant, questions become valuable.

When creation becomes easy, taste becomes scarce.

When intelligence becomes everywhere, human agency becomes the curriculum.

OpenLab believes:

- Students are builders, not passive users.
- Teachers are validators, not obsolete tools.
- AI agents assist, but they do not govern.
- Evidence must preserve the learning journey.
- Recognition should honor growth, not just output.
- Learning credentials for children must be non-financial, privacy-safe, and human-reviewed.

The goal is not artificial intelligence replacing learning.

The goal is human agency amplified by intelligence.
