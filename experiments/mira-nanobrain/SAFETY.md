# Research safety contract

1. All examples must be wholly invented. Pseudonymization alone is insufficient.
2. MIRA may support reflection, STEM inquiry and Socratic hints only.
3. AI-assisted Evidence Review is not Human Validation; no Learning Proof or Recognition is generated.
4. No grading, placement, discipline, eligibility, learner trait inference, emotional inference or behavioral profiling.
5. No model-generated shell commands, Python execution, network access, file writes or device flashing.
6. The allowlist and strict response shape are deterministic controls, not a guarantee that free-text output is safe. Before any live inference, add content review, threat modeling, adversarial testing, audit rules and human authorization.
7. Do not publish private student information, classroom incidents or identifiable learner work.
8. No classroom pilot or hardware deployment is authorized by this experiment.
9. Document all failures and uncertainty; never claim validation from passing a small unit-test suite.
