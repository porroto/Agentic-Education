# Agentic Education

Agentic Education is a learning model where students, teachers, families, communities, institutions, governments, and AI agents participate in a shared learning ecosystem while human purpose, ethics, civic responsibility, and proof-of-learning stay at the center.

## Core shift

Traditional education asks students to complete assignments.

AI-enhanced education often asks students to use AI to complete assignments faster.

Agentic Education asks students to build something real, use AI responsibly, show the process, prove the learning, and earn recognition for growth.

## Four forces

1. **Student as builder** — designs, tests, explains, revises, and presents.
2. **Teacher as validator-coach** — guides judgment, ethics, feedback, and meaning.
3. **AI agent as collaborator** — researches, drafts, simulates, organizes, questions, and documents.
4. **OpenLab as proof system** — preserves evidence through portfolios, rubrics, badges, logs, and validation.
