# Proof-of-Learning Framework

Proof-of-learning is a structured record of human growth demonstrated through evidence, reflection, iteration, and validation.

## Evidence categories

- Mission description
- Research notes
- AI-use disclosure
- Drafts and prototypes
- Data tables or observations
- Peer feedback
- Teacher feedback
- Revisions
- Final artifact
- Student reflection
- Human validation note

## Proof-of-learning claim

A proof-of-learning claim should include:

1. What the learner attempted
2. What evidence was collected
3. How the work changed
4. What the learner can explain
5. Who validated the evidence
6. What recognition was earned
