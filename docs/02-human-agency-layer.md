# Human Agency Layer

When intelligence becomes abundant, human agency becomes the curriculum.

## Human agency includes

- Purpose
- Choice
- Reflection
- Judgment
- Taste
- Ethical responsibility
- Persistence
- Revision
- Ability to explain decisions

## Why it matters

AI can produce many possible answers, but it cannot automatically decide which answer matters for a learner, community, classroom, or society.

OpenLab designs learning so students must show:

- What they chose
- What they rejected
- What changed their mind
- What evidence mattered
- What they would improve next
