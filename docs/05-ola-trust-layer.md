# OLA Trust Layer

OLA stands for OpenLab Assurance.

The OLA Trust Layer protects learning from becoming blind automation.

## OLA checks

- Is the evidence sufficient?
- Is the AI use disclosed?
- Is student privacy protected?
- Is the teacher still the final validator?
- Is the recognition non-financial?
- Is the badge based on growth, not just output?
- Are limitations or uncertainties documented?

## OLA principle

The loop should amplify teacher judgment, not replace teacher understanding.
