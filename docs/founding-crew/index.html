Our protected principles

Human first. Trust first.

🧭 Learner agency

Learners ask, create, explain, reflect, revise, and question AI suggestions.

🛡️ Human validation

AI may assist evidence review. A human educator makes the final learning validation decision.

✨ Meaningful recognition

Recognition follows validated evidence—not taps, XP, model guesses, or gameplay alone.
