# Ethics and Privacy

OpenLab must protect students before it scales.

## Rules

- Do not upload identifiable student data.
- Do not include student names in public repositories.
- Do not use student work for model training without appropriate consent and review.
- Do not allow AI to make high-stakes decisions.
- Do not issue financialized tokens to children.
- Do not hide AI involvement.

## Preferred data approach

Use synthetic data first.

Use de-identified data only when appropriate.

Use real student artifacts only with proper permission, school policy alignment, and ethical review.
