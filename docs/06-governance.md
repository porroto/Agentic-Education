# Governance

Agentic Education is not governed by AI. It is governed by human communities using AI responsibly.

## Governance roles

- **Students** govern their learning path, reflection, portfolio, and growth.
- **Teachers** govern pedagogy, validation, ethics, feedback, and evidence quality.
- **Families** govern consent, child dignity, cultural values, and privacy expectations.
- **Schools and districts** govern implementation, safety, equity, standards, and data protection.
- **Governments** govern public accountability, access, certification rules, child protection, and fairness.
- **AI agents** assist only. They do not govern.
